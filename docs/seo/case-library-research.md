# IT WHITE implementation library research

Updated: 2026-08-08

## Objective

Build an indexable library around durable business processes, not short-lived model names. Each page must answer a decision question for an owner or functional leader and remain useful if the underlying AI vendor changes.

## 2026-2027 demand signals

- AI agents are moving from standalone chat interfaces into controlled business workflows.
- Human accountability, audit logs, confidence thresholds and escalation are becoming buying criteria.
- Document AI remains a high-volume entry point because invoices, contracts and intake have measurable baselines.
- RAG is shifting from a generic knowledge bot to role-aware retrieval with citations, access control and document versions.
- CRM control, service routing and operational exception management are stronger commercial entries than broad transformation promises.
- Manufacturing demand concentrates around quality inspection, maintenance, technical knowledge and work-order control.
- Finance and professional services require provenance, review and explainability more than autonomous action.
- Management products are moving from static dashboards to exception briefs linked to primary events.

## Market language reviewed

The category and commercial language were compared with public pages from:

- KT.Team: process-first automation, AS-IS / TO-BE, AI assistants, RAG, quality control and architecture.
- KTS AI: assistants, agents, support, document automation, HR and RAG.
- PrimexAI: AI sales, voice, chat and CRM scenarios.
- Google Cloud customer stories: retail, supply chain, customer service, product discovery, quality inspection, finance and manufacturing.
- Microsoft industry stories: employee support, customer support, manufacturing and human handoff.
- UiPath: document understanding, invoice intake, validation, exceptions and human review.

Competitor material is used to identify demand patterns only. IT WHITE pages do not copy competitor wording, claim their results or present market scenarios as client work.

## Information architecture

Ten hubs, five scenarios each:

1. Sales and CRM
2. Support and service
3. Finance and documents
4. HR and professional services
5. Retail and e-commerce
6. Logistics and procurement
7. Manufacturing and construction
8. Real estate, medicine, education and hospitality
9. IT and security
10. Marketing and management

## Page quality gate

Every scenario must contain:

- a unique process problem;
- a target operating model;
- explicit human responsibility;
- systems and data sources;
- three measurable KPIs;
- a material risk or reason not to automate;
- a staged pilot plan;
- public evidence links;
- clear editorial labeling;
- one H1, self-canonical, Article and Breadcrumb schema.

## Indexing rollout

The technical release contains 50 complete pages. Search submission should be progressive:

1. Submit the hub and the first ten strongest pages.
2. Observe crawl, impressions, query match and engagement for four weeks.
3. Improve pages with impressions but weak CTR or poor query fit.
4. Submit the next sector cluster only after the first cluster is internally linked and quality-reviewed.
5. Add real IT WHITE cases beside research scenarios without changing their labels.

## Editorial rule

No generated percentage, client logo, production metric or implementation result may appear without an internal source. Market examples remain citations. IT WHITE delivery claims remain separate.
